This policy covers Arslan for iPhone (the App Store app) and Arslan for Mac (free and open source, from aralem.dev/arslan), and how the two work together.

01What we collect

Nothing. Neither app has analytics, tracking, advertising or an account. We, the developer, never receive your messages, files, tasks, approvals or any information about how you use Arslan.

02How your messages travel

When you send a message, a task or an approval from your iPhone, it is encrypted on the iPhone and saved as a record in your own iCloud private database (Apple CloudKit), under your Apple ID. Arslan for Mac, signed in to the same Apple ID, reads and decrypts it. Replies, progress and files travel back the same way.

The encryption keys are created when you pair the two devices and stay in the keychains of your iPhone and your Mac, so neither Apple nor we can read the contents. To deliver records, iCloud does see a few routing details next to the ciphertext: which of your devices sent a record and which should receive it, a sequence number, a coarse kind, whether it should notify you, and the time. Apple stores the encrypted records for you under Apple's privacy policy.

03AI processing happens on your Mac

Arslan for Mac runs the AI agent on your Mac. To answer you, it sends your requests to the AI model service you chose in its settings, with your own API key. That service processes them under its own terms and privacy policy. The iPhone app never contacts an AI service itself.

Before your first message, the iPhone app explains this and asks for your agreement. Nothing is sent before you agree, and you can withdraw your agreement at any time in the app's Settings › Privacy.

04Arslan for Mac

Your conversations, memory, files and settings are stored on your Mac. Arslan for Mac connects only to:

  • the AI model service you configured, with your key;
  • what a task you give it needs, such as the web pages it reads or a remote tool (MCP server) you connected;
  • your iCloud, for the iPhone link, only while Use Arslan from your iPhone is on;
  • GitHub, to check for and download updates of Arslan itself.

Each of those services sees what it is sent under its own terms. None of them is operated by us.

05Permissions on your iPhone

  • Camera — only to scan the pairing code shown on your Mac. Images are not stored or sent.
  • Microphone and speech recognition — to turn your voice into the text of a message, in the app's language. Recognition runs on your iPhone; recordings are not stored or sent.
  • Face ID or Touch ID — to confirm an approval. iOS handles it; the app only learns whether it succeeded.
  • Notifications — to tell you when your Mac needs an approval or has finished a task. Previews are decrypted on your iPhone, and you can turn them off in the app's settings.

06What each device keeps

On your iPhone

The pairing keys (in the iOS keychain), an encrypted local record of recent messages and tasks so the app can show them offline, your settings, and the device IDs this iPhone used in earlier pairings with each Mac (at most 8; not secret), so that pairing again replaces the old entry on your Mac instead of adding one. Unpairing removes the pairing keys and the local record.

On your Mac, about your phone

The phone's name, its public keys, when it was paired, and when the Mac last heard from it (to the minute). Settings › iPhone shows this as Connecting… or Connected · last seen ….

07Keeping and deleting data

  • Each record is deleted from your iCloud once the other device confirms it has received it.
  • Removing a phone on your Mac (Settings › iPhone › Remove) also deletes the messages the Mac had left in iCloud for that phone.
  • Pairing the same iPhone again replaces its old entry on the Mac, and what was waiting for the old one is deleted.
  • At start-up, the Mac deletes what is still waiting for phones that are no longer paired.
  • Anything else left over (for example, messages for a phone that stayed switched off) is deleted once it is more than 7 days old. The Mac checks about once a day while it is running and online; if it is off or offline for a while, the clean-up happens when it is back.
  • Unpairing on the iPhone (Settings › Unpair) clears the iPhone. To remove the phone from the Mac too, use Remove on the Mac. If you delete the app without unpairing, iOS may keep the pairing keys in the keychain; removing the phone on your Mac ends that pairing for good.
  • You can also delete the app's data from your iCloud in iOS Settings › your name › iCloud.

The clean-up on removing and re-pairing a phone needs Arslan for Mac 0.1.54 or later.

08Sharing, children, changes

We do not share any data with anyone. The AI model service is one you choose and contract with yourself; your Mac sends it your requests under that service's terms.

Arslan is not directed to children.

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Every change to this page is public in the project's history.

01我们收集什么

什么都不收集。两个 App 都没有统计分析、跟踪、广告或账号。开发者不会收到你的消息、文件、任务、批准，也不会收到任何关于你如何使用 Arslan 的信息。

02消息怎么传

你在 iPhone 上发出的消息、任务或批准，会先在 iPhone 上加密，再作为一条记录存进你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库（Apple CloudKit），归属于你的 Apple ID。登录同一个 Apple ID 的 Arslan Mac 版读取并解密它。回复、进度和文件按同样的路径传回来。

加密密钥在你配对两台设备时生成，只保存在 iPhone 和 Mac 的钥匙串里，所以苹果和我们都读不到内容。为了投递，iCloud 能看到密文旁边的少量路由信息：哪台设备发出、该由哪台接收、序号、粗略的类别、是否需要通知你，以及时间。苹果依照其隐私政策替你保存这些加密记录。

03AI 处理发生在你的 Mac 上

AI 智能体运行在你的 Mac 上。为了回答你，Arslan Mac 版会把你的请求发给你自己在设置里选择的 AI 模型服务，使用你自己的 API key。该服务依照它自己的条款和隐私政策处理这些请求。iPhone 版本身从不连接任何 AI 服务。

在你发出第一条消息之前，iPhone 版会说明这一点并征求你的同意。同意之前什么都不会发出；之后也可以随时在 App 的 设置 › 隐私 里撤回。

04Arslan Mac 版

你的对话、记忆、文件和设置都保存在你的 Mac 上。Arslan Mac 版只会连接：

  • 你配置的 AI 模型服务，用你自己的 key；
  • 你交给它的任务所需要的地方，比如它读取的网页，或你接入的远程工具（MCP 服务器）；
  • 你的 iCloud——只在 在 iPhone 上使用 Arslan 打开时，用于 iPhone 连接；
  • GitHub——检查并下载 Arslan 自己的更新。

这些服务各自按自己的条款看到发给它们的内容；没有一个由我们运营。

05iPhone 上的权限

  • 相机：只用来扫描 Mac 上显示的配对码。图像不保存，也不发送。
  • 麦克风与语音识别：把你说的话转成消息文字，识别语言跟随 App 的语言。识别在 iPhone 本机完成；录音不保存，也不发送。
  • 面容 ID 或触控 ID：用来确认批准。由 iOS 处理，App 只知道验证是否通过。
  • 通知：在 Mac 需要你批准、或任务做完时提醒你。通知预览在 iPhone 上解密，你可以在 App 设置里关闭预览。

06每台设备保存什么

iPhone 上

配对密钥（保存在 iOS 钥匙串里）、一份加密的近期消息和任务记录（方便离线时显示）、你的设置，以及这台 iPhone 以前和每台 Mac 配对时用过的设备 ID（最多 8 个，不是机密）——这样再次配对时，Mac 上会替换旧条目，而不是多出一条。解除配对会移除配对密钥和本机记录。

Mac 上关于你手机的信息

手机的名称、公钥、配对时间，以及 Mac 最后一次收到它消息的时间（精确到分钟）。设置 › iPhone 中显示为连接中…或已连接 · 某时在线。

07保留与删除

  • 每条记录在对方设备确认收到后，就会从你的 iCloud 里删除。
  • 在 Mac 上移除手机（设置 › iPhone › 移除），也会删除 Mac 留在 iCloud 里给这台手机的消息。
  • 同一台 iPhone 再次配对时，Mac 上的旧条目会被替换，等待旧条目的消息会被删除。
  • Mac 启动时，会删除仍在等待已不再配对的手机的记录。
  • 其他残留（比如发给一台一直关机的手机的消息），超过 7 天后删除。Mac 开着并联网时大约每天检查一次；如果 Mac 关机或离线了一段时间，等它恢复后再清理。
  • 在 iPhone 上解除配对（设置 › 解除配对）会清空这台 iPhone 上的数据；要从 Mac 上也移除，请在 Mac 上点“移除”。如果没有先解除配对就删除 App，iOS 可能仍在钥匙串里保留配对密钥；在 Mac 上移除这台手机，就能彻底结束这次配对。
  • 你也可以在 iOS 的 设置 › 你的名字 › iCloud 里删除这个 App 在 iCloud 中的数据。

移除和重新配对手机时的清理，需要 Arslan Mac 版 0.1.54 或更高版本。

08共享、儿童与变更

我们不与任何人共享任何数据。AI 模型服务由你自己选择并签约；你的 Mac 依照该服务的条款把请求发给它。

Arslan 不面向儿童。

如果本政策有变化，我们会更新本页和顶部的日期。本页的每次修改都在项目历史里公开可查。
