# 0.1.52 — Arslan remembers you, and learns from what worked

Design: `docs/specs/2026-10-03-0152-p4a-judgment.md` (task book P4a + the judgment layer skeleton; decisions D1–D5
as the task book recommends). Acceptance: `docs/plans/2026-10-03-0152-acceptance.md` (deepseek-v4-pro, $0.41).

## What was built

| Area | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Memory reaches your model | What Arslan remembers about you is used in conversations with the model you chose, cloud models included. Ordinary things it notices in your own words take effect at once, visible and undoable. Sensitive ones (health, money, where you live) still wait for your OK and the conversation's own permission. A temporary or "no memory" conversation stays as it was. Settings › Memory › "Remember me and use it in conversations" (on). Things noticed before this release are listed once to accept all or one by one. |
| Always in view | Two short sets are in every conversation: "About you" and "Arslan's notes about this Mac". Arslan edits them with `memory_note`; you pin or unpin entries in Brain. Arslan's own notes take effect at once only when that turn read nothing from the web, files or other outside content; otherwise they wait for you. |
| Past conversations | `conversation_search` finds your earlier words: original snippets with the date and a link that opens the conversation. |
| Learned practices | When you correct Arslan, or it fails one way and a different way works, it remembers the practice ("in this situation → do this") and uses it next time. A line in the chat says what it learned, with Undo. Brain lists them with where they came from and how often they were used and worked. A practice learned after reading outside content waits for your OK. Settings › Memory › "Learned practices take effect" (on). A practice never grants a permission. |
| Judgment layer | Small yes/no decisions asked of your own model (is this worth remembering, is it a duplicate, was it followed). Every answer is recorded with what really happened (Activity › Judgments). Command cards are judged in shadow only: every card still asks. |
| Fewer cards | With the command sandbox on, plain scripts (`python3 -c`, heredocs) run without a card; anything reaching the network, other apps or hidden code still asks. |

## Fixed

- The closing message at the hard work limit (0.1.50) never appeared in a real chat turn; it does now.
- Found by the acceptance runs: learning after the answer was refused by the finished turn; the judge timed out
  on thinking models; answer-style preferences were filed as practices; `swift` printed cache errors inside the
  sandbox when `$TMPDIR` pointed elsewhere.

## Measured

- A new conversation answered "what kind of answers do I like" from a preference said in an earlier one.
- Reminders: AppleScript refused once → Arslan learned "use EventKit" as a quirk of this Mac → the next run went
  to EventKit first, and the lesson was recorded as followed and successful. It was not faster: the saving is one
  quick failed call, smaller than run-to-run variation.
