# 0.1.51 — Arslan in the notch, and commands in a sandbox

Designs: `docs/specs/2026-10-03-arslan-island-research.md`, `docs/specs/2026-10-03-island-i1-plan.md` (Island) and
`docs/specs/2026-10-03-p3-command-sandbox.md` (P3). Measured on the kernel bench before shipping
(`docs/plans/2026-10-03-p3-acceptance.md`), deepseek-v4-pro.

## What was built

| Area | Files | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arslan Island | `desktop/src-tauri/src/island.rs`, `web/island.html`, `web/src/island/`, `server/services/desktop_status.py`, `server/api/desktop.py` | A small Arslan at the top of the screen, in the notch (a short bar on screens without one). <br>• While work runs: the mascot, the plan count or elapsed time. Hover for the task, its plan and the last steps. <br>• It opens by itself when something needs your OK, shows "done" for a few seconds, and tells you when a task stopped. <br>• States are shown by the mascot's mouth (signals, three orbiting dots, !, ω, a flat line, small and dim when you are away); there are no sounds. <br>• Never in screenshots, recordings or screen sharing. Click-through everywhere except the island itself. <br>• Settings → Desktop → "Show Arslan in the notch" (on). Approving from the island comes in a later release. |
| Command sandbox | `server/services/command_sandbox.py`, `terminal_exec.py`, `tool_loop.py`, `ws/arslan.py`, `approvals.py`, `RunCommandCard.tsx` | Commands Arslan runs can read files and use the network, but write only inside its working folder, temp and cache folders, and can never read your SSH keys, the keychain files or Arslan's own data and keys (macOS seatbelt; the protected paths are closed last, so they hold even inside writable folders). <br>Leaving the sandbox is always your click: <br>• Arslan asks up front with a reason, in one card. <br>• A command the sandbox stopped can be run again outside it. <br>• A checkbox allows it for the rest of the conversation; this is kept in memory only. <br>Settings → Advanced → "Run commands in a sandbox" (on). |

## Measured (deepseek-v4-pro, T1/T2/T3/T5 × 3, against Hermes)

| Task | 0.1.51 Arslan | Hermes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reminders (T1) | **3/3**, $0.025, 76 s | 2/3, $0.058, 142 s |
| Job table (T2) | 1/3, $0.167, 313 s | 3/3, $0.093, 160 s |
| Apple financials (T3) | 3/3, $0.041, 77 s | 3/3, $0.026, 36 s |
| Organise Downloads (T5) | 3/3, $0.053, 113 s | 3/3, $0.046, 100 s |

The sandbox raised **no card in 12 runs**. Confirmation cards averaged 1.0 per run, against 0–7 (mostly 0–2) before.

## Known gaps (next)

- T2: research still finds too few jobs in some runs. Script cards (`python3 -c`, heredoc) no longer protect anything
  the sandbox does not, and they move into 0.1.52 as "no card while sandboxed".
- Apple Events (`osascript`, Shortcuts) act through other apps and are not confined by the file sandbox. They keep
  their own card.
- Island: click-through on a real screen and showing over another app's full-screen window are not yet verified; a
  click on the island brings Arslan forward (a non-activating panel comes with approving from the island).
