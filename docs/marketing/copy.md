# Social media copy

Ready-to-post promotional copy for Arslan, in English and Chinese. Three hooks
run through everything: **a machine, not a chat box** · **self-evolution that
must pass an exam** · **all local, your keys**. The tone matches the product's
"honest by design" brand — pre-v1 and macOS-first are never hidden.

**Suggested pairings:** main posts → demo GIF (`docs/assets/demo.gif`) or the
social card (`docs/assets/social-preview.jpg`); safety angle → FIG. 01/02

### Show HN

> **Show HN: Arslan – a local AI orchestrator whose self-evolution must pass
> an exam**
>
> I've been building a personal AI orchestrator that runs entirely on your
> machine with your own LLM keys. You talk to one host agent; it delegates to
> persona "spawns" you configure. The part I care most about: spawn prompts
> rewrite themselves from run history, but a candidate only surfaces after
> beating the incumbent on held-out past tasks — on every dimension — and
> nothing takes effect until you click Promote. Memory is a visible "second
> brain" where every belief carries time, and model-proposed deletes go to an
> inbox instead of applying silently. Pre-v1, macOS-first (the kernel sandbox
> is seatbelt-only for now), Apache-2.0. Honest limitations are in the README.

### 公众号 / 掘金 — 长文导语

> 远不会见到；你拒绝的版本，现任继续在岗。这不是免责声明式的"安全"，是工程
> 化的信任。

### V2EX / 技术社区发帖

> 标题：做了个本地优先的 AI 编排器：自进化要过考试、代码跑内核沙箱、记忆带
> 时间轴，求拍砖
>
> 正文：个人项目，Apache-2.0。几个设计点：
> ① 单一入口主控 agent，按任务路由给你自己配置的 persona 分身（工具/技能包/
> MCP 都能挂）；
> ② 分身提示词自动进化，但候选版必须在 held-out 历史任务上全维度 ≥ 现任才能

> token 不进沙箱，没有沙箱环境直接 fail closed；
> ④ 第二大脑是 FTS5+向量混合检索的图谱，信念带生效/被取代时间，可以把图谱拨
> 回任意历史时刻看。
> 目前 pre-v1、macOS-first，限制都写在 README 里了。
