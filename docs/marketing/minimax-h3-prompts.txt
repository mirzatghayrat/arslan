NOTES
=====================================================================

Syntax: the bracketed camera command must sit at the very front with NO space
between the closing bracket and the first word. Three movements maximum.

Order to generate in, if budget is limited: 3 (EMBLEM) first, then 6 (SETTLE),
then 1 (WAKE). Shot 3 replaces a move that is currently faked by pushing 3.4x
into 720p footage, and shot 6's last frame is the first frame of F8-Silk.

Do not prompt for the Arslan interface — no composer, no ledger, no dashboard,
no laptop with the app on it. The model will produce a confident, plausible,
fictional Arslan. Real screens come from video/public/rec/, which holds twelve
2560x1680 screenshots of the actual client.

If a monitor, laptop or caption appears in a take anyway, discard the take
rather than re-rolling with heavier negative weighting.
