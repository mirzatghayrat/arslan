Arslan is an AI agent that lives on your Mac. It runs your terminal, drives your apps and keeps working in the background while you talk — and keeps you posted from the notch, or from your iPhone. Anything that acts in your name waits for your click.

01How it works

One loop. Every step needs the one before it.

A message enters one native tool loop. The model proposes; a fixed policy — not another model — decides. Every step below names the file that enforces it.

    02The gate · try it

    What “it runs your terminal” actually means.

    Every shell command gets one of three answers before it runs. Type one, or pick from the list — each of these 64 commands was run through Arslan's actual policy function. Nothing here is hand-labelled.

    arslan · terminal_policy.assess()
    ↑ ↓ to move
    —
    pure function · decided before it runs
    RUN

    Reading, listing, converting, building, fetching a page — inside a sandbox that can only write to your work folder, temp and caches.

    ASK

    Destructive, or acting outward in your name: deleting, force-pushing, other apps, mail, installs. Tick “don't ask again” for that kind of command.

    FORBID

    Never, even with a tick: wiping the disk or home folder, shutting down, keychain passwords, Arslan's own keys, sudo.

    04Hands new in 0.1.53

    Hands for your Mac. Asked, not assumed.

    Arslan can read and use the apps on your Mac — Notes, Mail, Pages and the rest — through a small helper, Arslan Hands. It reads a window as its accessibility tree, never a screenshot, and works in the background: your mouse, keyboard and front window stay yours.

    Mac apps Arslan Hands

    Looking at an app asks once per app in a conversation. Acting happens only in background work and asks once per app.

    05Mac + iPhone

    Your Mac does the work. Your pocket holds the leash.

    Arslan for iPhone talks to the Arslan on your Mac — from anywhere, through your own private iCloud, encrypted end to end.

    coming to the App Store

    Arslan for iPhone

    Ask from anywhere, follow every job, and approve or deny in one tap. The Mac side ships in 0.1.53 — Settings › iPhone.

    No server of ours. Not even a relay.

    Messages travel through a CloudKit zone in your private iCloud database. Apple carries only ciphertext; delivered messages are deleted, and Arslan for Mac clears anything left over once it is more than 7 days old — it checks about daily while it is running and online.

    Honest limitations

    • Pre-v1. macOS 11+ on Apple Silicon only, for now.
    • Bring your own model key — your provider bills you.
    • The gate guards against mistakes and smuggled instructions; it is not a cage. A command you allow runs with your permissions.
    • The sandbox limits where commands write, not where they connect — Arslan reads the web with it.
    • Hands sees windows on the current desktop only, and a large window (Notes with many notes) can take 10–20 seconds to read.
