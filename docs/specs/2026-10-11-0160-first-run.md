# First run, redone — one head, six shots, a camera

Status: flow (§2) approved by the user 2026-10-10 ("流程可以"); look "1+2" (the black-and-white head with
the constellation mouth, with a film's feel: "到位的设计感使得产品有信任感"); method A, code-rendered
("A 代码实时渲染"). The visual language (§3) was presented the same night and not objected to; the boards
were drawn on the design canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/RMddAsC3eyBGT9BVzzjvmi (a playable prototype + every
shot in dark and light). The user then handed over the night ("放手干 交给你了"): this spec and its build were
done without the user seeing the boards — the PR says so, and nothing merges into a release they have not seen.

## 1. Why

The wizard from 0.1.30-era (`web/src/components/FirstRunWizard.tsx`) is a frosted panel over a looping clay
character video, a four-beat "how it works" tour from the experts era, a capability score per provider
("tool calling 3/5"), and three random outro clips. It does not look or speak like Arslan today (one assistant,
the black-and-white head, Hands, the iPhone), and it never asks the two questions that decide what Arslan can do
on day one: may it read your folders, and may it use your apps.

go away after the first message or when dismissed.

The × (skip setup) stays, top right, as today; Esc still does not skip. Finishing or skipping sets
`first_run_seen` exactly as today, so the gate (`firstRunShouldShow`) is unchanged.

## 3. Look

- **Stage:** the window is the screen. Dark: near black `#08090C`; light: paper `#F6F6F3` — following the app's
  theme. One soft key light behind the head (upper right), a vignette, very fine film grain (an SVG noise
  filter, a few % opacity). No gradients elsewhere, no glass.
- **The head is the island's head** — `IslandMascot` with its mouth engine, unchanged. Dark stage: white face,
  black eyes and mouth (as on the island). Light stage: the brand's light-surface treatment, black face, white
  eyes and mouth (a new `tone="ink"` on `IslandMascot`; the island keeps its default). State colours only in
  the mouth and its halo: blue signals, amber "!", green ω.
- **Camera:** each shot has a head position and scale (a slow push in, a pan); between shots the head moves in
  ~1.1 s (ease in-out) while the text cross-fades. Reduced motion: no camera, no morph, fades only.
- **Type:** the system font. A small mono eyebrow ("02 — A MIND TO THINK WITH"), one 60 px title, one muted
  line, at most two buttons. Text sits on the left like a subtitle; the head on the right.
- **Hand-off:** on Start the head travels to the sidebar brand mark's real position (measured), shrinks to its
  size, and the stage fades out over the live app.

## 4. Build

- `FirstRunWizard.tsx` rewritten; same props and the same App wiring. `firstRun.css` rewritten. The videos in
  `web/public/first-run/` (bg, poster, three outros, ~9 MB) are removed.
- `web/src/island/mascot.css` holds the mascot's styles (moved out of `island.css`, which imports it) so the
  wizard can use the same head; `IslandMascot` gains `tone`.
- Folders: `api.updateSettings({ default_read_enabled })`. Hands: `getHands` (availability), `askHandsPermission`,
  `checkHands`. Model: the existing OpenRouter OAuth and `testLlm`/`addProviderConfig` paths, unchanged.
- First tasks: the wizard records which were turned on (`localStorage`, best effort); the chat's empty state
  shows the chips until the first message.
- Six languages for every new line; the removed tour/outro/capability keys go.

## 5. Tests (each must fail on today's wizard)

- Walk: language → model → folders → (Hands when available) → name → finish; the language pill switches at once
  and persists.
- Folders: no card is selected on arrival; each card writes its value; there is no way forward without one.
- Hands: hidden when unavailable; Open asks for the prompt; a later check that says allowed advances by itself;
  Not now skips.
- Model: OpenRouter sign-in, its error and paid-fallback notices (existing behaviours kept); the key form is
  folded until asked; a failing key shows the real error and save-anyway.
- Finish: the name is saved; seen is set; with reduced motion it closes at once; skip (×) sets seen.
- Chips: shown after finish for what was turned on; a click fills the box without sending.
