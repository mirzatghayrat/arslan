# Hands in 0.1.60: the paragraph for the release notes (English; ②Main places and translates it)

## Arslan Hands v2: using your Mac apps

Arslan can now see and use your Mac apps much more like a person does, and asks less often.

- **It sees the window.** Besides the window's text, Arslan can look at a picture of the one window it is
  working in (never the whole screen, never saved). Turn this off in Settings → Hands, or list apps that are only
  ever read as text.
- **Fewer steps, fewer waits.** Several steps in one app run as one batch, menu items are chosen directly (even
  when the app is not in front), and apps are opened in the background.
- **Asking, once.** One card can cover several apps for a piece of work, a background job started from a
  conversation inherits that conversation's yes, and apps you trust can be allowed for good in Settings → Hands.
  Deleting, sending, paying and submitting still ask every time.
- **Borrowing the front, only when needed.** A few things only work with the app in front, such as a pop-up
  menu. Arslan waits until you pause typing, takes the front for about a second (the screen edge glows blue),
  keeps the keys you press meanwhile and gives them back, then returns your app. You can turn this off.
- **Taking over, with your yes.** For longer work Arslan can ask to use the screen for up to 30 minutes. The edge
  glows the whole time, and the moment you touch the keyboard or mouse it stops and waits for you. "While I'm
  away" (off by default) lets it start after three minutes without your input.
- **The island shows it.** The island shows what Hands is doing: waiting for your pause, the front borrowed, a
  takeover with time left and Stop, and a thumbnail of the window being worked on.
- **Short tasks in the chat.** A chat reply can now act directly, up to five steps; longer work moves to the
  background.

Fixed:
- A stale macOS app record (an app that never finished launching) no longer makes every app look "not running".
  Arslan Hands now says when it cannot get the app list, and the engine skips such a record.
- Apps that bring themselves forward after an action (Notes on New Note) are put back behind your app.
- A card in a chat reply can be answered from the chat window. Before, it was only answerable from the island or
  the Inbox (fixed with the chat-liveness change).

Known limits:
- Clicking at a screen position and dragging are not offered yet.
- When an app brings itself forward (Notes), it is in front for about 50 ms before Arslan puts it back. A key
  typed in that moment goes to that app.

## For the morning report (not for users): what is not verified

- **G10 / L3 has never run with a model.** Spec §15 A8 makes it a gate for P2. The first pilot found the card bug
  (fixed by #160); the second never reached the model (the user's metering proxy answered 500 to every request).
- The chat-window card fix (#160) is proven by tests only (tests/server/test_hands_card_in_turn.py), not yet in a
  real model run.
- Settings → Hands, the island's Hands panel and the locked-screen check have not been looked at on the real Mac.
- The look median (with a screenshot) sits at its 400 ms line: 385–409 ms across runs, 617 ms under heavy load.
- Release smoke on main `4ac18c0c`: 30/31 (the one failure is that look median, 409 ms).
- The engine's stale-record fix is a patch carried in our fork, offered upstream (lahfir/agent-desktop#266).
