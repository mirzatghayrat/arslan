## Arslan 0.1.55 — one look for everything that asks, and pages that say what they are for

**Every card, dialog and notice looks and behaves the same.** Approval cards, confirmations, dialogs, toasts and warnings now share one design in both themes:
- **⌘⏎ allows, Esc declines.** Plain ⏎ never answers a card, so typing in the composer cannot approve anything by accident.
- **Cards that wait stand in one queue** ("1 / 3") above the composer, oldest first, with the time left.
- **Scripts say what they change.** An AppleScript or command card shows what Arslan says it does, and a separate line read from the code itself when it deletes, moves, sends or runs a shell command. The code decides; the summary never does.
- **Deleting asks first.** Deleting a provider, an SSH key or machine, a paired iPhone, an MCP server, a note, a conversation, a scheduled task, a watch, a memory or a learned practice — and resetting the access token — now asks before it happens; a saved candidate can be undone for a few seconds instead.

**Arslan Island v2.**
- **Answer in the island.** A waiting card can be allowed or declined right in the island. A step that may delete, send, pay or submit shows "Open in Arslan" instead, so you read it in full first; this rule is checked by Arslan itself, not only hidden in the island.
- **Over a full-screen app** a waiting card is a small, still tab — "Waiting for you" and the time left. Point at it to open the card.
- **While work runs** the island shows how long it has been running, a Stop button for background work, and Open Arslan.

**Inbox = what waits for your decision.** Approvals, stopped work, pages and folders that changed, and memories that wait for your OK, in one place; watches and mutes are managed here too.

**Settings in seven places.** General · Models · What Arslan may do · Background · Memory & privacy · Connections · About. Every setting kept a home; old links still land on the right page. About shows the version and what changed.

**Memory as one page.** What waits for your OK, what is always in view (with its real size limit), what Arslan knows about you — most recently used first, with "used 3× this week" — and the practices it learned, with their record. Materials, this week's use and who can use your memory sit on the right. The graph is unchanged; its controls float on the canvas.

**Capabilities as switches.** One list of what Arslan can do, grouped by what it does. Each row is on, off, one step away (one button: add a key, connect, open Settings) or not possible here, with the reason. Off means Arslan does not see it at all. A saved method (skill) can now be switched off without deleting it.

**Fixed**
- Arslan knows its own version and can tell you what changed in recent releases.
- When Arslan remembers something during a conversation, it says so in the thread, with Undo; near-duplicates are no longer saved twice.
- The tool steps of a reply show above the answer, in the order they happened.
- A first message is no longer taken as a correction, and a correction that contradicts something that just worked waits for your OK instead of taking effect at once.
- Skills without content no longer crowd out usable ones (Arslan saw 31 of 44); skills imported from a repository read the skill's own license file.

**Known limit:** clicking the island still brings Arslan to the front.
