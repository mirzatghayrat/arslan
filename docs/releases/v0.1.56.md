## Arslan 0.1.56 — projects in two layers: a board for all of them, levels for each

**A board for every project.** Four columns — Idea, Shaping, Doing, Done. Where a card sits follows the level the project is on; nothing is dragged. Each card shows what "done" means, how many levels are left and how long it has been on this one. Dropped projects fold under Done; a list view shows them all.

**Levels for each project.** Pick a type (game, app, website, research, writing, video, skill, trip, job, other) and a finish line, and the levels are drafted from the type at once, without a model. "Let Arslan refine" adapts them to your finish line with one call on your own model, only when you press it. Each level has a clear condition and a few checkpoints; a checkpoint can name the file that shows it is done (for example `levels/*.json`).

**Arslan ticks what it can see, and asks before moving on.**
- **Files:** when the named files appear in the project folder, the checkpoint is ticked ("2/3" while they arrive).
- **What you said:** "the fun test went great" ticks that checkpoint, with your words as the evidence.
- **Runs:** "Hand to Arslan" opens a project conversation with the next step typed (not sent); a background job there that finishes ticks the checkpoint.
- **Clearing a level is a proposal** on the card — move on, or not yet. Every tick and every move says why and can be undone. Done and Dropped are only ever yours. Nothing here is a notification or an Inbox item.

**Arslan learns how you move projects.**
- After 10 right guesses in a row it asks once whether it may move levels itself (also in Settings › Background). With that on, two undos in a row and it offers to stop.
- "See what it learned": the plan rules it drafts with — a level you add to two projects of a type, work you cut mid-way twice, the line you add when you say "not yet" — each with a switch and where it came from; and your pace per level, from three cleared levels on.
- A project quiet for three weeks (or twice your usual) says so on its card — pick it up, pause, change the plan — and never more than that.

**When the direction changes,** Arslan can propose a changed plan in the conversation; the card shows what changes, and cleared levels never do.

**Done:** Arslan offers a short retro — what took longer than usual, how the plan changed, and up to three rules you can keep for next time. One call, only if you want it.

**Fixed (Hands)**
- An action that timed out could run a second time; each request now runs at most once.
- Appending to a field whose text could not be read no longer replaces the field — it refuses.
- "Submit" presses Return only when the field itself has focus.
- A wait for text that timed out now says so; an action that was sent but not confirmed says "sent, not confirmed" instead of done.
- The cursor label follows the interface language.

**Known limits:** a project's plan rules are used when drafting, not yet when Arslan proposes a changed plan. Clicking the island still brings Arslan to the front.
