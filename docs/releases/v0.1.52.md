## Arslan 0.1.52 — remembers you, and learns from what worked

**Memory reaches your model.** What Arslan remembers about you is used in conversations with the model you chose, cloud models included. Ordinary things it notices in your own words take effect at once, visible and undoable. Sensitive ones (health, money, where you live) still wait for your OK. Temporary and "no memory" conversations stay as they were. Settings › Memory › "Remember me and use it in conversations".

**Always in view.** Two short sets are in every conversation: "About you" and "Arslan's notes about this Mac". Pin or unpin entries in Brain; Arslan can add notes too, and they wait for your OK when that turn read anything from the web or files.

**Past conversations.** Arslan can find what you said before — original words, the date, and a link that opens the conversation.

**Learned practices.** When you correct Arslan, or one way fails and another works, it remembers the practice and uses it next time. A line in the chat says what it learned, with Undo; Brain lists them all. A practice never grants a permission. Settings › Memory › "Learned practices take effect".

**Fewer cards.** With the command sandbox on, plain scripts run without asking; anything reaching the network or other apps still asks.

**Fixed.** The closing message at the work limit now appears in real chat turns.

### Measured (deepseek-v4-pro)

- Reminders: after AppleScript was refused once, Arslan learned "use EventKit" as a quirk of this Mac and went there first next time; the lesson was recorded as followed and successful. It was not faster in this test (the saving is one quick failed call).
