## Arslan 0.1.60 — Hands v2: your Mac apps, used more like you would

**It sees the window.** Besides a window's text, Arslan can look at a picture of the one window it is working in — never the whole screen, never saved. Turn it off in Settings → Hands, or list apps that are only ever read as text.

**Fewer steps, fewer waits.** Several steps in one app run as one batch, menu items are chosen directly (even when the app is not in front), and apps open in the background. A chat reply can act directly for up to five steps; longer work moves to the background.

**Asking, once.** One card can cover several apps for a piece of work, a background job started from a conversation inherits that conversation's yes, and apps you trust can be allowed for good in Settings → Hands. Deleting, sending, paying and submitting still ask every time.

**Borrowing the front, only when needed.** For things that only work with the app in front, such as a pop-up menu, Arslan waits until you pause typing, takes the front for about a second (the screen edge glows blue), keeps the keys you press meanwhile, then returns your app. You can turn this off.

**Taking over, with your yes.** For longer work Arslan can ask to use the screen for up to 30 minutes. The edge glows the whole time, and the moment you touch the keyboard or mouse it stops and waits. "While I'm away" (off by default) lets it start after three minutes without your input. The island shows what Hands is doing, with time left, Stop and a thumbnail of the window.

**Fixed:** an app that never finished launching no longer makes every app look "not running"; apps that jump forward after an action (Notes on New Note) are put back behind yours; unpairing on the iPhone now removes the phone on the Mac too.

**Known limits:**
- Clicking at a screen position and dragging are not offered yet.
- An app that brings itself forward is in front for about 50 ms before Arslan puts it back; a key typed in that moment goes to that app.
