## Arslan 0.1.61 — one approval card everywhere, MCP servers that start themselves, Claude works again

**Claude works again.** With Claude Opus 4.7 and later, including the default claude-sonnet-5 and claude-sonnet-5-5, Arslan no longer answers every request with a 400 error saying `temperature` is deprecated. The key was fine: Arslan sent a setting these models refuse, and now sends it only to the models that accept it. When a provider stops accepting an optional setting in future, Arslan removes it, sends the request again and remembers that for the model, so this does not need a new release. Not yet verified with a real key: the fix was tested against the providers' documented error replies, so the first real check is you pressing Test in Settings.

**MCP servers start by themselves.** Every server you switched on starts in the background when Arslan opens, at most four at a time, and a chat never waits for one. Each server has one state that the Capabilities page and the model both read, and the model is offered only servers that are ready right now; a call to any other answers with its state and why. A failure says why, with keys and tokens masked. A first start gets 120 seconds to download and launch; a failed server is tried again after 30 seconds, then less often. Connect is a fresh start.

**One approval card everywhere** — in the chat window, the Inbox, the island and on the iPhone.
- The choices are Once or For this task (whichever the card offers first), In this conversation, Always, and No… with a note that goes to Arslan. A message you type in the chat while its card waits answers it No, with your message as the note, and runs next.
- "In this conversation" says what it covers: a kind of command, any scheduled task, any file in the folder.
- A card waits 30 minutes, and running out is not a no: Arslan is told nobody answered, not that you refused. Before, it was declined after 5 minutes.
- One click answers one card. For a moment after a card comes up, a second click or a held key cannot answer the next one.
- Hands asks once for looking at an app and acting in it, for this task first; deleting, sending, paying and submitting still ask every time. Every AppleScript still asks every time, with the whole script shown.
- What could not be asked while you were away (a card that never opened, and most of what a scheduled task was refused) is listed in the Inbox with exactly what "Always allow" would store, and it can be undone for 10 seconds.
- The island and the iPhone show the card's own question and say what their yes means; they answer once, or for this task where that is the card's first choice, and never "Always".

**iPhone: files and live conversations.** Files and photos you send from Arslan for iPhone are saved on the Mac in your working folder under Inbox/iPhone, and Arslan reads them with your message. The phone lists your live conversations, follows them while Arslan works, and can delete one. A history too long for one message loses its oldest messages and says so. The privacy page now says what travels, where it lands on the Mac and what the phone keeps. The Mac side works with Arslan for iPhone 1.0, which is coming to the App Store.

**The web behind a fake-IP proxy, and page reads that name themselves.** On a Mac whose proxy or VPN answers DNS with fake addresses, Arslan can read web pages and use its browser again: the proxy resolves the name, and Arslan still refuses local names, real private addresses and, in that setup, anything but https on port 443. A page read now says who is reading, so sites that turn away unnamed clients (Wikipedia answered 403) answer; when a site still refuses, Arslan is told the site blocks automated reading, not that a key was rejected.

**Fixed:**
- Stopping a task while it writes to the database no longer keeps the lock for seconds, so other writes no longer fail with "database is locked".
- Apps that share a name (WeChat and its helper, Claude and the Claude Code sessions) are told apart, so looking at an app no longer fails because the name is shared; a window title that does not exist falls back to the app's only window or lists the titles; table rows and fields no longer make a click "risky" because of what they contain.
- A "for this task" yes ends with its task, also after a restart or when the task is resumed.

**Known limits:**
- Commands still ask as in 0.1.60. Fewer command cards, and commands that stay in the sandbox first, are planned for 0.1.62, not in this version.
- Card titles are in English.
- "Always" for sites and Shortcuts is hidden for now: those cards offer For this task and In this conversation. It comes back with the Settings page that can list and remove such rules.
- "In this conversation" cannot be taken back yet: there is no list, and it lasts as long as the conversation. The Settings page that lists and removes it comes in a later version.
- Going back to 0.1.60 after upgrading needs the automatic backup in `backups/`. 0.1.60 will not open the new database.
- The island no longer answers AppleScript cards; they are answered in Arslan.
