## Proactivity: Arslan looks out for things, and you decide

Arslan 会替你留意事情，放进新的「收件箱」；它只发现和提议，你点「去办」才会动手。

Arslan now keeps an eye on a few things while it runs and leaves what it finds
in a new **Inbox** in the sidebar:

- **Background jobs that stopped short** — out of budget, needing your input,
  interrupted. **Do it** picks the work up again with the same standards it had.
- **Scheduled tasks that keep failing**, with the last errors quoted.
- **Web pages you ask it to watch.** It raises an item only when a page
  meaningfully changes (at most once a day per page) and shows the lines that
  were added and removed.
- **Folders you ask it to watch** (inside your workspace), with the new file names.
- An optional **morning brief**: what is open, what is running, and which
  scheduled tasks run in the next 24 hours. Skipped when there is nothing to say.

Every item says *why*, with the evidence. Text that came from a page or a file
is shown in quotation marks and is never followed as an instruction.

**Nothing runs until you choose Do it.** Do it starts a background job in a
conversation you can watch; that job still asks before it writes files, runs
commands or acts on a website. You can also **Snooze** an item (a day, three
days, a week) or mark it **Not useful**, optionally silencing that page or that
kind of notice for good. Silenced things are listed in Settings and can be
brought back.

Looking costs nothing: detection never calls a model. With **Quiet
notifications** on (the default), a watched page or folder that changed, or a
ready brief, shows a short system notice — never what was found, so nothing
private appears on a locked screen — at most five a day, and none in your quiet
hours (22:00–08:00 by default). Clicking it opens the Inbox.

Settings → **Proactivity** holds what to look at, notifications, quiet hours,
the brief and your watches.

### Optional, off by default: a guess at the cause

Settings → **Automation** has a daily dollar limit for "guess the cause of
problems". When set, a new problem in the Inbox gets a short guess at what
happened and what to try, from your cheap model, labelled as a guess. A guess is
only made if its worst-case price fits what is left of today's limit, and never
with a model whose price Arslan does not know.

## Sidebar

The **Conversations** entry and the second **+** beside "Recent conversations"
are gone: the conversation list is the way back to a conversation, and **New
conversation** at the top is the one way to start one.
