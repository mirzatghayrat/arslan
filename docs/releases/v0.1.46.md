## Settings: a compact side list, and only the right pane scrolls

设置页改成紧凑的侧栏：九个分组一屏看全，右侧内容自己滚动，整页不再一起滑动。

- The left list is smaller type and tighter rows, so every entry fits in one
  screen. It stays where it is while you scroll.
- The right pane scrolls by itself and slides in briefly when you change
  section (off when your system asks for reduced motion).
- The "This app" version box moved to the bottom of the left list.

## Fixed: background self-improvement of experts

If you had switched on **Auto-evolution** before experts were removed in 0.1.44,
Arslan could still spend in the background rewriting expert prompts that nothing
reads any more. It no longer does. (The switch itself disappears when the
expert code is removed in a later version.)
