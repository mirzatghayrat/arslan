## Behaviour change — read first

**Closing the window no longer quits Arslan.** It stays in the menu bar and
keeps working: tasks you started and scheduled tasks keep running. To quit,
press ⌘Q or choose **Quit Arslan** in the menu-bar menu. Clicking the Dock icon
or **Open Arslan** in the menu bar brings the window back.

关窗不再退出 Arslan：它会留在菜单栏继续工作。要彻底退出，请按 ⌘Q 或在菜单栏选择「退出 Arslan」。

- While work is running, Arslan keeps the Mac from going to sleep on its own
  (Settings → Desktop, on by default). The display can still sleep; closing
  the lid or low battery still puts the Mac to sleep.
- While the window is closed or in the background, macOS notifies you when a
  task finishes, when an action needs your confirmation, and when a scheduled
  task fails or is paused (Settings → Desktop, on by default). The first
  notification asks for macOS permission. Notifications never show message
  text; a scheduled task is named by its own label.
- An action that needs your confirmation still waits in the window, and is
  still declined if nobody answers within 5 minutes — hidden window or not.
- Scheduled tasks missed while the Mac was asleep are not replayed (unchanged).

## Source review of saved reports (optional, off by default)

Settings → Automation → *Source review of saved reports*. When on, after
Arslan saves a report that cites several web sources, one extra model request
rereads the sources and flags claims such as "document A doesn't mention X"
that a source may contradict. The report is always saved first; the notes
appear when you open it and are model advice, not a fact check. Only official
DeepSeek and Anthropic connections are supported; other models are skipped.
The 0.1.40 blocking review gate is removed.

A calibration on 13 retained reports found 1 correct hint out of 14 (2 more
borderline) and missed both known in-scope errors. It stays off by default
until a redesign is re-measured.

## Known limitations

跨语言或多文档对比时，模型可能错误地断言某份文档缺少某项内容；请打开来源核对。来源链接用于导航，不是事实核查。

The 0.1.40 R4 limitation is unchanged: cross-language comparisons can wrongly
claim that a document lacks something. The optional source review does not fix
it (see the calibration above).

## Upgrade

0.1.41 adds no database migration, so upgrading from 0.1.40 creates no
automatic upgrade backup. Upgrading from 0.1.38 still runs the 0.1.40
migrations and their pre-upgrade backup, as described in the 0.1.40 notes.
