Arslan Support / Arslan 支持

English

Contact: support@aralem.dev — or open an issue at https://github.com/mirzatghayrat/arslan/issues

What do I need? Arslan for Mac (free, from https://aralem.dev/arslan/) on a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 11 or later, and an iPhone with iOS 17 or later, both signed in to the same Apple ID with iCloud turned on.

How do I pair? On your Mac, open Arslan › Settings › iPhone, turn on "Use Arslan from your iPhone" and click "Add iPhone". On your iPhone, open Arslan and scan the code. Confirm the request on your Mac.

My Mac shows as offline. Make sure the Mac is awake, Arslan is running and the switch above is on, both devices use the same Apple ID, and both are online.

How do approvals work? When the agent on your Mac wants to do something outside its own folder or sandbox, it asks first. Approve with Face ID or Touch ID, or decline, on the phone or on the Mac — the first answer counts. Voice can ask, but only you approve. A card nobody answers expires after five minutes.

Can I try it without a Mac? Yes: on the first screen, tap "Try the demo".

Voice input: hold to talk; it listens in the app's language (English or Chinese) and turns your voice into text on the iPhone. You review the text before sending.

How do I unpair? On the Mac: Settings › iPhone › remove the phone. On the iPhone: Settings › Unpair.

Privacy: https://aralem.dev/arslan/privacy

中文

联系我们： support@aralem.dev，或在 https://github.com/mirzatghayrat/arslan/issues 提 issue

需要什么？ 一台 Apple 芯片、macOS 11 或更高版本的 Mac，装好 Arslan Mac 版（免费，https://aralem.dev/arslan/），以及 iOS 17 或更高版本的 iPhone；两台设备登录同一个 Apple ID，并打开 iCloud。

怎么配对？ 在 Mac 上打开 Arslan › 设置 › iPhone，打开“在 iPhone 上使用 Arslan”，点“添加 iPhone”。在 iPhone 上打开 Arslan 扫码，再到 Mac 上确认。

Mac 显示离线？ 确认 Mac 没有睡眠、Arslan 在运行且上面的开关已打开、两台设备是同一个 Apple ID、并且都联网。

批准怎么用？ Mac 上的智能体要在它自己的文件夹或沙箱之外做事时，会先来问你。可以在手机或 Mac 上用面容 ID 或触控 ID 批准，或者拒绝，以先到的回答为准。语音可以提要求，但只有你能批准。没人回应的批准卡五分钟后过期。

没有 Mac 能先试试吗？ 可以：在第一个页面点“先看看演示”。

语音输入： 按住说话，识别语言跟随 App 的语言（中文或英文），在 iPhone 本机转成文字，你确认后再发送。

怎么解除配对？ Mac 上：设置 › iPhone › 移除这台手机。iPhone 上：设置 › 解除配对。

隐私政策： https://aralem.dev/arslan/privacy
